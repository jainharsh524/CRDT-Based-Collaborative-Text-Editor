## Notes

- Detect/converge poll numbers include the **2s `stat()` sleep**; that is the legacy watch path, not merge CPU.
- Converge `n` is successful trials only (timeouts are dropped, not counted as slow samples). kqueue succeeded 7/12; poll succeeded 3/4.
- Span-vs-line savings are for short (≈1 character) edits on ~75-character lines.
- LWW timestamps are `clock_gettime(CLOCK_REALTIME)` nanoseconds (not 1s `time()`).
- Snapshot buffers use C++17 `atomic_load` / `atomic_compare_exchange` on `shared_ptr`.
- Registry updates are serialized with `flock` (fixes the multi-process `user_count` race).
- Re-run `./CRDT --bench` on another machine before citing these numbers.
